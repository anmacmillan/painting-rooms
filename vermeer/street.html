The Girl in Delft

one walk through Delft, about 1665 · after Johannes Vermeer

Girl with a Pearl Earring

Johannes Vermeer painted her in Delft about 1665. Nobody knows who she was. The museum that keeps her calls the picture a tronie: a painting of an imaginary figure, not a portrait.

You ran past The Little Street and looked in on The Milkmaid. He lived on the Oude Langendijk, where you ended, and the streets are the real ones.
