# Vermeer's Delft: findings

All sources fetched 13 November 2026 by a research agent. EV = essentialvermeer.com (a specialist site, not a museum).

## Verified

- Number of paintings. 34 are "universally attributed"; 34 firm plus three in question on the list page; nl Wikipedia says 34 to 37. The Frick says "about thirty-four". The Vermeer Centrum shows 37 reproductions.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Vermeer ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_paintings_by_Johannes_Vermeer ; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Vermeer ; https://collections.frick.org/objects/273 ; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermeer_Centrum_Delft
- Girl with a Pearl Earring, c. 1665, is a tronie: "not a portrait, but a 'tronie' – a painting of an imaginary figure", a girl in exotic dress and turban. The Mauritshuis says there "would probably be a sitter"; it names nobody. Wikipedia: the identity is disputed; the suggestion that she is his eldest daughter Maria is dismissed by other art historians as an anachronism.
  https://www.mauritshuis.nl/en/our-collection/artworks/670-girl-with-a-pearl-earring/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girl_with_a_Pearl_Earring
- Light. Wikipedia says his works are "usually domestic interiors with one or two figures lit by a window on the left". No source fetched gives a count.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Vermeer
- View of Delft, c. 1660-1661: the town across the Kolk, a triangular harbour, with the Schiedam and Rotterdam gates and the Nieuwe Kerk tower between them. Vermeer moved the tower, lengthened the bridge and lowered the Rotterdam Gate.
  https://www.mauritshuis.nl/en/our-collection/artworks/92-view-of-delft/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/View_of_Delft
- What survives. The Kolk keeps its shape. The Nieuwe Kerk and Oude Kerk stand; the Nieuwe Kerk spire dates from 1875. Both gates and the town wall went in the 1830s; "not a single stone remains". The house Vermeer painted from is gone. OpenStreetMap still maps the Armamentarium.
  https://www.essentialvermeer.com/catalogue/view_of_delft.html ; https://www.essentialvermeer.com/delft/delft_today/view_of_delft.html
- Buried in the Oude Kerk, 15 December 1675. Grew up at the Mechelen inn on the Markt (demolished 1885, nothing on the site). Lived on the Oude Langendijk with his mother-in-law.
  https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Vermeer ; https://www.essentialvermeer.com/delft/delft_today/mechelen.html
- The Little Street at Vlamingstraat 40-42 is Frans Grijzenhout's 2015 proposal.
  https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Het_straatje
- The Oostpoort is the only one of eight gates left.
  https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oostpoort_(Delft)

## Unverified or in conflict

- How many interiors have the window on the left: no source.
- "Hooikade" as the viewpoint: no text source names it; the map shows Hooikade on the Kolk's south bank.
- Which corner of the Molenpoort held Vermeer's house: east (now the Maria van Jessekerk) by tradition, west (Oude Langendijk 25) on H.G. Slager's research.
  https://www.essentialvermeer.com/delft/delft_today/oude_langendijck.html
- Gate demolition years: Schiedam Gate 1834 (EV) or 1836 (nl Wikipedia); Rotterdam Gate 1834-1836.
- Guild house demolition: 1876, 1879 or 1886, by source.
- Museum dates not checked at the museum: The Lacemaker (Louvre), The Music Lesson (Royal Collection), and the two National Gallery of Art pictures (dates from NGA's Commons donation). Those sites refused automated requests.
- The NGA page URL for A Lady Writing in casting.json was not opened.
