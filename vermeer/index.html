The Girl in Delft

Delft, about 1665 · inside the paintings of Johannes Vermeer

Girl with a Pearl Earring

Johannes Vermeer painted her in Delft about 1665. Nobody knows who she was. The museum that keeps her calls the picture a tronie: a painting of an imaginary figure, not a portrait.

He lived his whole life in this town and left only about thirty-four paintings. These are the ones you looked in on.
