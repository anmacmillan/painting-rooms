Garden
Why not take the chance to wander through the over ​​22,000 plants in Rubens's garden? The ​​advice of Antwerp fashion designer Dries Van Noten ensures color across all seasons in a design by Ars Horti. Move around, view and savour the scents of this unexpected patch of greenery in the very heart of Antwerp. 
©Jeroen Broeckx
The artist's residence is temporarily closed due to renovations. Discover Rubens Experience, the garden and the library.

More about the garden
Step into Rubens's garden and you will be overcome by the 22,000 plants, trees, shrubs and flower bulbs. Take time to breathe, just as Rubens himself did when he came into this patch of greenery in between painting commissions and as a break from everyday life.
