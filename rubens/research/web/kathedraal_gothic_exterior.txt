Exterior
Above the main entrance to the cathedral you will spot a monk pointing at a skull in his lap. He reminds you that you too will die. One floor above him, the Archangel Michael points out that your soul will be weighed.
We are put on this earth for a reason. If you want to achieve the highest level and come home to Christ, then enter to discover which actions in life will determine your fate at the Last Judgement.
