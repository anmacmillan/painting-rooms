Majestic
A Gothic church interior is conceived to immerse visitors in the sublime, in beauty, in perfection, in the eternal.
The first thing you’ll notice about this church is the huge space that is created by the combination of length, width and height. Regardless of where you’re standing in the cathedral, its full circumference is never revealed to you.
In our cathedral, you will get a taste of infinity!
