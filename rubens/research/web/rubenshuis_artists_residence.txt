Artist's residence
Rubens converted his house in Antwerp into an Italian city palace. He lived, worked and died here. The artist's house is currently closed for restoration. 
©Ans Brys
The artist's residence is temporarily closed due to renovations until 2030. Discover Rubens Experience, the garden and the library.
Rubens was a citizen of the world, but he always returned to his beloved Antwerp. He bought a house there and converted it into an Italian city palace. Here he lived life to the fullest and found inspiration for his masterpieces. The artist's house is currently closed for restoration and will reopen in 2030 at the earliest. You are already welcome to visit the Rubenshuis for Rubens Experience, the garden and the library.

Rubens Experience
Who was Rubens really? Discover the artist and the man behind the brushes. His story really comes to life in the Rubens Experience, with immersive projections, interactive tools, and masterpieces by Rubens and van Dyck. The icing on the cake? You find yourself face to face with the man himself thanks to his famous Self-Portrait.

Garden
Why not take the chance to wander through the over ​​22,000 plants in Rubens's garden? The ​​advice of Antwerp fashion designer Dries Van Noten ensures color across all seasons in a design by Ars Horti. Move around, view and savour the scents of this unexpected patch of greenery in the very heart of Antwerp.
