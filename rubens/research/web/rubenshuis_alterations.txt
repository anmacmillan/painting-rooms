The Rubenshuis is more than a museum. Because Rubens was more than a painter. The museum is undergoing a radical transformation. The universe of the idiosyncratic artist will be given more breathing space. The renovation and reopening will take place in phases. The historical artist's residence is temporarily closed for restoration. Rubens Experience, the library and the garden are already buzzing with activity. Get to know the renovated Rubenshuis.

Alterations in phases
The Rubenshuis has been undergoing a major transformation since 2016 and the work will not be completed until 2030. The alterations and the reopening are being carried out in phases. At the same time, the ambitions of the new concept are becoming clearer – improved accessibility, more space, extra facilities and maximum comfort and convenience, both for visitors and for the collection itself.

Artist's residence
Rubens converted his house in Antwerp into an Italian city palace. He lived, worked and died here. The artist's house is currently closed for restoration.

Rubens Experience
Who was Rubens really? Discover the artist and the man behind the brushes. His story really comes to life in the Rubens Experience, with immersive projections, interactive tools, and masterpieces by Rubens and van Dyck. The icing on the cake? You find yourself face to face with the man himself thanks to his famous Self-Portrait. 
Read more
Garden
Why not take the chance to wander through the over ​​22,000 plants in Rubens's garden? The ​​advice of Antwerp fashion designer Dries Van Noten ensures color across all seasons in a design by Ars Horti. Move around, view and savour the scents of this unexpected patch of greenery in the very heart of Antwerp.
