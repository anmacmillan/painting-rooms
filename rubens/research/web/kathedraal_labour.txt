Labour, art and faith
Various altarpieces shed light on the patronage of the trade corporations, guilds and fraternities in the 16th and 17th centuries.
They also give us a unique insight into how the inextricable relationship between the Church and society developed over time.
Look at the tools that the bakers requested be sculpted into the vaults: a sublime tribute to the human labour that is praised in heaven!
