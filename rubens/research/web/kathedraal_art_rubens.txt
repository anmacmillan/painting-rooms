4x Rubens at a glance
When you set foot in the heart of our cathedral, you will be met by a unique sight. Here you can see three masterpieces by Rubens.
Perhaps this may not seem special until you realise that two of them are in situ. The Descent from the Cross and The Assumption of the Virgin Mary were painted for the building in which they are still on display today. You will not find this anywhere else in the world. Together with The Elevation of the Cross, they are
the highlight of any visit to Antwerp.
Find further information about 'Flemish Masters in situ' and about the Rubens paintings in Antwerp Cathedral here!
