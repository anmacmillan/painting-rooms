## Changes from the brief that the facts required

1. The train heads west, so the run starts at Slough and the Windsor field comes before the bridge, on the left (south).
2. No "carriages" to run over: the sources describe this train as open wagons only. Seven goods wagons and five open passenger trucks stand in for them.
3. No moving rods (see above). Wheels turn; the chimney puffs four times to each turn of the driving wheels.
4. The steam is small puffs, not clouds, as the Gallery describes.
5. The end card says the hare can no longer be seen in the painting.
